## What is a store or restaurant?

A store is any seller on the app: restaurants, grocery, convenience, alcohol, health, retail, pet, flowers, baby, personal care and electronics. Each store has its own menu or catalog, delivery fee and open or closed switch.

## What is a delivery partner?

A delivery partner is the person who collects an order from the store and brings it to the customer. Partners go online or offline from their dashboard.

## What is a vendor?

A vendor is a store owner who manages the store in the vendor portal: opening and closing it, the menu, the delivery fee and incoming orders.

## What does "Placed" mean?

The customer has submitted the order and the store has not responded yet. It is step 1 of 6.

## What does "Ready" mean?

The store has finished preparing the order and it is waiting for a delivery partner.

## What does "Partner assigned" mean?

A delivery partner has taken the order and is heading to the store.

## What does "On the way" mean?

The partner has picked up the order and is bringing it to the customer.

## What does "Rejected" mean?

The store declined an order while it was still Placed. The payment is marked refunded.

## What does "Cancelled" mean?

The order was cancelled automatically because its payment failed. Customers cannot cancel orders themselves.

## What is the delivery fee?

A per-store charge added to the subtotal. The total is the subtotal plus the delivery fee.

## What is promo text?

A short label a store can show on its card, such as an offer line. It is for display and does not change the total.

## What is Mock Card, Mock UPI and Cash on Delivery?

The three simulated payment methods. Mock Card and Mock UPI succeed about four times out of five. Cash on Delivery always succeeds. No real money moves.

## What is an option group?

A set of choices a vendor attaches to a menu item, such as size or toppings, with a minimum and maximum number of choices. Customers pick from them when adding the item to the cart.

## What does it mean when a delivery partner is online or offline?

Online partners can receive orders and see available ones. Offline partners cannot receive orders.

## What is Suspend on the admin Vendors page?

An administrator action that pauses a store. A suspended store is switched to closed and customers cannot order from it. Unsuspend lifts the pause.

## What is Zippy?

Zippy is the in-app help assistant. It answers questions about using the app and also about stores, dishes, prices and whether a store is open (for any visitor, no restriction by role). It can look up a signed-in customer's own orders, but it cannot place, change, cancel or pay for orders. It can also prepare cart changes for a signed-in customer to confirm, and show a Go to checkout card that opens the Checkout page, where you enter your details and pay yourself. It never places orders, pays or cancels.
