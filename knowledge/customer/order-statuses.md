title: What each order status means
---

## How many steps does an order go through?

The tracker has six steps: Placed, Accepted, Preparing, Ready, On the way, Delivered. Two statuses (Partner assigned and On the way) share step 5. Rejected and cancelled orders show a red banner instead of the tracker.

## What does "Placed" mean?

Placed is step 1 of 6. The message reads "Order placed — waiting for restaurant". You have submitted the order and the store has not responded yet.

## What does "Accepted" mean?

Accepted is step 2 of 6. The message reads "Restaurant accepted your order". The store will start preparing it, and you receive an email.

## What does "Preparing" mean?

Preparing is step 3 of 6. The message reads "Restaurant is preparing your order".

## What does "Ready" mean?

Ready is step 4 of 6. The message reads "Order ready for pickup". The order is waiting for a delivery partner, who is normally assigned within seconds.

## What does "Partner assigned" mean?

Partner assigned is step 5 of 6. The message reads "Delivery partner assigned". A delivery partner has taken the order and is heading to the store.

## What does "On the way" mean?

On the way is also step 5 of 6. The message reads "Order picked up — on the way". The partner has collected your order and is bringing it to you. Your order page then plays a 15-second courier animation, after which the order becomes Delivered.

## What does "Delivered" mean?

Delivered is step 6 of 6 and the final state. The message reads "Delivered". The order page shows a green status and you receive one delivered email.

## What does "Cancelled" mean?

Cancelled shows a red banner reading "Order cancelled" instead of the tracker. It is a final state. An order is cancelled automatically when its payment fails. You cannot cancel an order yourself.

## What does "Rejected" mean?

Rejected shows a red banner reading "Restaurant rejected your order — payment refunded". It is a final state: the store declined the order while it was still Placed, and your payment is marked refunded.

## Does the order page keep updating?

Yes. The order page refreshes by itself while the order is in progress and stops once it reaches Delivered, Cancelled or Rejected.
