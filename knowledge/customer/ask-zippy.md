## What can Zippy tell me about stores and menus?

Zippy can look up stores and dishes for you. You can ask whether a store is open right now, what a dish costs, what options a dish has, which stores offer free delivery, which stores are rated highest, or which stores serve a type of food. Zippy checks the current store and menu information when you ask, so the answer reflects what the app shows at that moment.

## Are the prices and open status Zippy gives me up to date?

Yes, they are looked up when you ask. Stores can change a price, mark a dish unavailable, or open and close at any time, so an answer can be out of date a few minutes later. Before you order, check the store status. Checkout always re-checks the current prices and tells you if something changed since you added the item.

## Can Zippy find the store nearest to me?

Yes. On the website, Zippy uses the delivery location you chose (or the default location if you have not chosen one) to rank stores by distance. In the mobile app, Zippy uses your phone's location: the first time you ask about nearby stores, your phone asks permission to share it while the app is open. If you say no, Zippy cannot sort by distance but can still answer about ratings, delivery fees and open stores. Zippy only uses the location for that question and does not save it with your chat.

## Can Zippy see my orders or place an order for me?

Zippy can look up your own orders when you are signed in as a customer: the status of an order, what was in it, what it cost, how it was paid and where it is being delivered. Zippy reads the order when you ask. It cannot see anyone else's orders, and it cannot place, change, cancel or pay for an order. It can open the Checkout page for you with a card, but you enter your details and pay yourself. If you are not signed in, Zippy cannot see any orders.

## Can Zippy add things to my cart?

Yes, when you are signed in as a customer. Ask Zippy to add a dish, add the items from a past order again, change a quantity, remove an item or clear the cart. Zippy shows a card describing exactly what it will do, and nothing changes until you tap Confirm. You can tap Dismiss instead. Zippy cannot add dishes from a closed store or dishes that are unavailable, and each item can be added up to 20 at a time. If a card adds a dish from a different store than the one in your cart, confirming it replaces the items currently in the cart (the card says so). Zippy does not place or pay for the order. When your cart is ready, ask Zippy to check out and it shows a Go to checkout card that opens the Checkout page for your current cart. Zippy refuses if the cart is empty, the store is closed or a dish is unavailable. On the Checkout page you enter your own name, contact details and address and pay yourself; Zippy never places or pays for an order and never asks for those details or card numbers in chat. In the phone app the Confirm button always works. On the website the cart cards work on the customer pages (on other pages the card shows a link to the customer area instead, so open your cart from there), and the Go to checkout card works wherever the website shows your cart (the customer pages); on other pages (vendor, admin or delivery) Zippy cannot see your cart, so open the customer area and ask again. The phone app always works.

## Why does Zippy take a few seconds to answer a store question?

Zippy looks up the live store and menu details before it answers, and the whole answer appears at once when it is ready instead of word by word. A question that needs a lookup usually takes several seconds.
