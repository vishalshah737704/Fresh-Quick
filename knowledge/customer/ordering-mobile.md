## What is in the Customer app on my phone?

After you sign in as a Customer, the app has three tabs at the bottom: Home, Orders and Account. The Delivery Partner app is separate and is for delivery partners only.

## How do I find a store in the mobile app?

1. On the Home tab, use the search pill ("Search restaurants or dishes") to search by store name, cuisine or even the name of a dish.
2. Or tap a cuisine chip in the row of chips; tap the same chip again to clear it.
3. Home also shows promotions and an "Order again" row with stores you ordered from before, if you have any orders.
4. Tap a store card to open the store.

Pull down on the list to refresh it.

## How do I add items to my cart in the mobile app?

1. Open a store.
2. Tap the round + on an item to add it straight to your cart.
3. If an item has options such as size or add-ons, tapping it opens a pop-up sheet where you choose them first. Required choices must be made before you can add it.
4. You can add a special-instructions note to an item in that same step.

Once your cart has items, a floating "View cart" pill appears above the tab bar. Tap it to open the cart.

## Can I order from more than one store at once?

No. A cart holds items from one store only. Adding an item from another store asks you to clear the current cart first.

## What is in the cart on the phone?

The cart is its own screen titled Cart. It has quantity buttons, Remove, a note for each item, an order note for the whole order, Clear cart, and the subtotal, delivery fee and total. Tap Checkout when ready. The tip buttons are for show only: choosing a tip does not change your total and no tip is charged.

## How do I place an order in the mobile app?

1. In the cart, tap Checkout.
2. Check your contact details. Name and email may be filled in from your account. The phone number is required: a 10-digit Indian mobile number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9, with optional +91, 91 or 0 in front.
3. Open Delivery address and fill in Address line 1, City, State and Pincode (Address line 2 is optional). There is no map picker; you type the address.
4. Optionally open Delivery instructions and add a note.
5. Open Payment method and choose Mock Card, Mock UPI or Cash on Delivery. All are simulated and no real payment is taken.
6. Tap Place order. The button stays disabled until all required fields are valid.
7. You are taken to the order screen to follow it.

## How do I see my orders in the mobile app?

Open the Orders tab. It lists your orders, newest first, with the store, status, date and time, item photos and the total. Tap one to open its details and tracker. If you have none it says "No orders yet."

## What does the order screen show?

At the top are the order number, the status and a one-line message. Below is a six-step tracker (Placed, Accepted, Preparing, Ready, On the way, Delivered) with a caption such as "Step 3 of 6 · Preparing". Then come your delivery details, the items, the totals and payment, and a timeline of times. While your order is assigned or on the way, a map shows the store (S), your delivery address (H) and, when the partner has reported a position, the partner (D), with a dashed straight line between them. Below it the screen shows when the location was last updated and about how far the partner is from your address; a location older than 5 minutes is marked as possibly out of date. The distance is a straight-line estimate, not the road route, and the phone shows no arrival time (the website shows a road route and an estimated time). After delivery the map stays as a final view. The map uses the phone's own maps (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android); if it cannot be shown, the partner's coordinates are shown as text. The screen refreshes by itself every few seconds until the order is finished.

When a delivery partner is assigned, a card appears with "Delivery partner" and Call and Message buttons. These two buttons are not connected yet and only show a message.

## What if my payment fails in the mobile app?

The screen shows "Payment failed. Your order was not placed — please try checking out again." Go back to your cart and check out again.

## How do I cancel an order?

You cannot cancel an order yourself; the app has no cancel button for customers. If the store cannot take your order while it is still Placed, it rejects it: the order shows Rejected and your payment is marked refunded. An order is also cancelled automatically if the payment fails.

## Will I get an email about my order?

Yes. An email goes to the address you entered at checkout when the store accepts your order and again when it is delivered.

## What is the Account tab for?

It shows your name and email, a Reset password link, Wallet (a read-only list of the payment methods available), Help (a short FAQ) and Sign out.
