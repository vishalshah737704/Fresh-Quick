## The Place order button is greyed out. What is missing?

The button stays disabled until every required field is valid. Check your name, email, phone number (a 10-digit Indian mobile number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9), Address 1, City, State, Pincode, and the fields for your chosen payment method.

## I see "Session expired — please log in again" at checkout. What now?

Your sign-in is no longer valid. Log in again and go back to checkout. Your cart items are still there.

## Checkout says the restaurant is currently closed. Can I still order?

No. The store has switched itself to closed, so checkout is refused until it reopens. Try again later or order from another store. A store an administrator has paused shows "This restaurant is currently unavailable." instead.

## Checkout says prices have changed. What should I do?

The message reads "Prices have changed since you added items to your cart. Please review your order." Open your cart, check the new prices and place the order again.

## Checkout says an item is no longer available. What should I do?

The store has marked that item unavailable. Remove it from your cart and place the order again.

## My payment failed and the order shows Cancelled. What do I do?

Mock Card and Mock UPI fail at random about one time in five, and a failed payment cancels the order automatically. Place the order again, or choose Cash on Delivery, which always succeeds.

## I cannot sign in. What should I check?

An error message appears below the form when the email or password is wrong. Re-enter both. Use the customer sign-in page, because store owners and delivery partners have their own pages. If you have no account yet, choose "Need an account? Sign up". The password must be at least 6 characters.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

The sign-in screens have no forgot-password link; Reset password is available only while you are signed in. If you are signed in, open the account menu (website) or the Account tab (mobile app) and tap Reset password under your name. A reset email is sent to your account address and the link changes to "Reset email sent".

## My order is stuck on Placed. Is something wrong?

Placed means the store has not responded yet. Wait for the store to accept it. If the store rejects it, the order shows Rejected and the payment is marked refunded. You cannot cancel it yourself.

## The order page is not updating. What should I do?

The page refreshes by itself while the order is in progress, so just keep it open. If it still looks stuck, reload the page or reopen the order from Orders.

## I do not see my order in the list. Where is it?

Orders lists every order placed from the account you are signed in with, newest first. Make sure you are signed in with the same email you ordered with.

## I did not get an order email. Why?

Emails go to the address entered at checkout when the store accepts the order and when it is delivered. Check that address for typos and look in your spam folder.
