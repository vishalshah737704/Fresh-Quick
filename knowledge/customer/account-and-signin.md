## Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse every store and category without an account. You need an account only when you check out.

## How do I create a customer account on the website?

1. On the customer sign-in page, switch to Sign up.
2. Enter your details and choose a password of at least 6 characters.
3. Submit the form to create your account and continue.

## How do I create a customer account in the mobile app?

1. Open the app and choose Customer on the first screen.
2. Tap "Need an account? Sign up".
3. Enter your full name, your email and a password of at least 6 characters.
4. Tap Sign Up. You are signed in automatically.

Tap "Have an account? Log in" to go back to signing in.

## Can I use the same account on the website and the phone?

Yes. The same email and password work on both, and an order placed on one shows up on the other.

## How do I sign in?

1. Open the customer sign-in page on the website, or choose Customer in the mobile app.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Tap the sign-in button (Log In in the mobile app).

If your email or password is wrong, an error message appears below the form.

## Why am I signed out when I open the app?

The mobile app does not remember your last session. Each time you open it from scratch you pick your role and sign in again. Switching to another app and back does not sign you out. The website also asks you to sign in again after the service has been restarted.

## How do I reset my password?

1. Open the account menu on the website (the hamburger icon in the top right of the header) or the Account tab in the mobile app.
2. Tap Reset password under your name.
3. A password-reset email is sent to your account address, and the link changes to "Reset email sent".
4. Open the email and follow its link to choose a new password.

## How do I sign out?

On the website open the account menu and choose Sign out. In the mobile app open the Account tab, tap Sign out and confirm.

## What is in the account menu on the website?

After you sign in, the hamburger icon in the top right opens a menu with your name and a Reset password link, Orders, Wallet (the payment methods available at checkout), Help (a short FAQ) and Sign out.

## I see an error on my order page after signing in elsewhere. What do I do?

Signing in as another kind of user (for example a store owner) in another tab of the same browser can replace your customer sign-in. Sign in again as the customer. The order page sends you back to the sign-in screen when this happens.

## Can I create a delivery partner or admin account from the customer app?

No. Delivery partner accounts are added by an administrator, and admin accounts are not self-service. Store owners and delivery partners use their own sign-in pages.
