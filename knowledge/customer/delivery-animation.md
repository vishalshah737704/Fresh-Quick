## What happens when my order is picked up?

Delivery partners only mark an order as picked up; they cannot mark it delivered. When your order is On the way, your order page opens a "Your order is on the way!" window with an animated courier scene and a countdown that reads "Arriving in 15s". This works the same on the website and in the mobile app.

## How long does the animation last?

It always plays for a full 15 seconds from the moment the window first appears, even if the partner picked up the order a while ago. It plays regardless of your device's reduce-motion setting.

## What happens when the countdown ends?

1. The window briefly shows "Confirming delivery…".
2. It then shows "Delivered! Enjoy your meal." and your order becomes Delivered.
3. The window closes by itself after about 3 seconds. There is no Done button.
4. One delivered email is sent to the address you entered at checkout.

## What if I leave the order page during the animation?

If you come back while the app or browser session is still open, the animation carries on from where it left off.

## What if the app could not confirm delivery?

The window says "We couldn't confirm delivery just yet — your order will be marked delivered shortly." and shows a Close button. The order is then completed automatically.

## What if I never open the order after pickup?

Nothing is lost. The system marks the order Delivered by itself about 20 seconds after pickup and sends the same single delivered email. The order page does not need to be open.

## Will I get more than one delivered email?

No. An order is delivered exactly once, whether it completes through the animation or automatically, and you get one delivered email.

## Why did my order become Delivered by itself?

Partners only mark pickup. After pickup, the 15-second animation finishes and the order is marked Delivered, or the system does it about 20 seconds after pickup if you did not open the order.
