## How do I create a delivery partner account and sign in?

On the delivery sign-in page choose "New partner? Sign up". Enter your full name, choose your vehicle (Bike, Scooter, Bicycle or Car), then an email and a password of at least 6 characters, and choose Sign up. To sign in later, enter email and password and choose Log in. An administrator can also add a partner account.

## What do I see on the Dashboard?

The Dashboard has an Online or Offline button, a section called "Your active deliveries" and a section called "Available orders". The left menu has Dashboard and History, your name with a Reset password link, and Sign out.

## How do I go online or offline?

Choose the button that reads "Offline — tap to toggle" or "Online — tap to toggle". To start getting deliveries, go online with this button: you only receive orders and see available orders while you are online.

## Why do I see no available orders?

The list says "Go online to see available orders." while you are offline, and "None right now." when nothing is waiting. Orders are normally assigned automatically to the nearest online partner, so an order often goes straight to your active deliveries without appearing in Available.

## How are orders assigned to me?

When a store marks an order Ready, the order is assigned automatically to the nearest online partner, based on the location your device last reported. Keep your location accurate while online.

## How does my location get reported?

While you are online the Dashboard shows two boxes with your latitude and longitude. It fills them from your device if you allow location access, and keeps sending your position every 15 seconds. You can type values yourself if location is blocked.

## Can I accept an order myself?

Yes. If an order is still waiting under Available orders, choose Accept on its card. If another partner got it first you see "Order is no longer available to claim". You must be online to accept.

## What do I see about the customer?

Before you accept, an available order shows the store and items, and the card says customer details appear after you accept. Once the order is yours, the card shows the drop-off name, phone, address and any delivery note. A phone number starting with + is a tap-to-call link.

## How do I pick up an order?

When the store hands you the order, choose "Mark picked up" on the card in "Your active deliveries". The order becomes On the way.

## Can I mark an order as delivered?

No. After you mark it picked up, the card reads "Customer is receiving the order…". The customer's order page plays a short delivery animation and then completes the delivery. If the customer never opens the order, it is completed automatically shortly afterwards.

## Where can I see my past deliveries?

Open History in the left menu. It shows your 50 most recent finished orders. Choose Refresh to reload.
