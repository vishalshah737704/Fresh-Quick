## What information does the app ask for when I order?

At checkout you give your name, email, phone number and delivery address, and you can add a delivery note and notes on items. Your name and email are used to place the order and to send order emails.

## Who can see my phone number and address?

The store sees your name, phone number and delivery address on the order. A delivery partner sees them only after taking your order, and only while it is active. Before that, a partner browsing available orders sees the store and items, not your details.

## Are my payment details stored?

No real payment details are stored. Payments are simulated, and the app says so on the Wallet page.

## Does the app remember my details for next time?

The name, email and address you type at checkout are not saved in your browser for later. Only your chosen delivery location (the pin and its label) is remembered, so the store list matches your area. The mobile app also asks you to sign in again each time you open it from scratch.

## Where can I find the terms of use or privacy policy?

The app does not include a terms of use or privacy policy page. Zippy cannot quote terms that are not in the app.

## How do I contact support?

The app does not list a support phone number or email address, so Zippy cannot give you one. In the app, the Help page in the account menu answers common questions.
