## Is this app taking real money?

No. All payments in this app are simulated. No real money moves and no real card or bank details are stored. The Wallet page lists the payment options available at checkout and says the same.

## Which payment methods are available?

Mock Card (16-digit card number, MM/YY expiry and cardholder name), Mock UPI (a UPI ID such as name@bank) and Cash on Delivery (no extra details).

## Why did my payment fail?

Mock Card and Mock UPI succeed or fail at random, succeeding roughly four times out of five. A failure does not mean anything is wrong with your details. Cash on Delivery always succeeds.

## What happens to my order if the payment fails?

The order is cancelled automatically and shows a red "Order cancelled" banner. Go back to checkout and place the order again.

## How do I get a refund?

You cannot request a refund yourself. When a store rejects your order while it is Placed, the order page reads "Restaurant rejected your order — payment refunded" and the payment is marked Refunded. Because payments are simulated, no real money is returned.

## How do I see how an order was paid?

Open the order page. It shows how the order was paid, with the payment status (for example Refunded) and the payment method, next to the subtotal, delivery fee and total.

## Is my total ever different from what I saw in the cart?

The total is the subtotal plus the store's delivery fee. If a store changes an item price after you added it to your cart, checkout stops and shows "Prices have changed since you added items to your cart. Please review your order." so you can review before paying.

## Can I add a tip?

The app has no tip option at checkout.
