## Can I order from more than one store in a single order?

No. A cart holds items from one store only. If you add an item from another store, the app asks whether to clear your cart and start a new one.

## Can I cancel an order after placing it?

No. Customers have no cancel button. The only ways an order ends early are these: the store rejects it while it is still Placed (the order shows Rejected and the payment is marked refunded), or the payment fails and the order is cancelled automatically.

## Can I change the items or address after I place an order?

No. The app has no screen for editing an order once it is placed. Check your cart, delivery address and phone number carefully before you select Place order.

## How is the delivery fee decided?

Each store sets its own delivery fee. You can see it on the store card ("Delivery Fee"), in your cart panel and at checkout, and it is added to the subtotal in your total. A store with a fee of 0 shows "₹0 Delivery Fee" in green.

## What is the promo text on a store card?

Some stores show a short promotional label, such as an offer line, on their card. It is a label set by the store. It does not change your subtotal or total, which are worked out from item prices and the delivery fee only.

## Why can I not order from a store?

The store page shows a red notice. "This restaurant is currently closed." means the store has switched itself to closed. "This restaurant is currently unavailable." means an administrator has paused the store. You cannot check out from either until the store is open again.

## How do I know when my order is on the way?

Open your order page. The six-step tracker moves to On the way when a delivery partner has picked your order up. It refreshes by itself, and you also get an email when the store accepts the order and when it is delivered.

## Who delivers my order and what do they see?

A delivery partner takes your order once it is Ready. Orders go automatically to the nearest online partner, so you do not choose one. Partners see your name, phone number and delivery address only after they have taken the order, and only while it is active.

## Can I track the delivery partner on a live map?

Yes, on the website. Once your order is assigned or on the way, the order page shows a map with the store, your delivery address and the delivery partner. While the partner is on the way, a solid line follows the roads to your address and the page shows an estimated time and road distance, for example "About 23 min · 8.3 km by road". The time is the usual driving time without live traffic, so treat it as an estimate. If the road route cannot be loaded, a dashed approximate straight line is shown instead, with no time estimate. The map refreshes by itself with the latest reported location of the partner. The phone app shows a simpler map with a straight dashed line and no arrival time.

## How long will my order take?

The app does not promise a delivery time. Store cards show an average preparation time in minutes, and the home page has an Under 30 min filter based on it. The tracker shows the real progress.

## Can I leave a review for my order?

Yes, once the order is Delivered, on the website and in the phone app. You rate the store (required), and can also add a comment, one photo, ratings for dishes and a rating for the delivery. Each order can be reviewed once and a customer cannot edit or delete a review. Reviews do not trigger refunds. For a wrong or missing item contact support, see the refund policy.

## Who can see my review?

Everyone who opens the store's page can see it: your first name and last initial, stars, comment, photo and dish ratings, plus any reply from the store. The store can reply to it or report it. The delivery rating and its comment are seen by the delivery partner without your name, and the Fresh & Quick team can see them too.
